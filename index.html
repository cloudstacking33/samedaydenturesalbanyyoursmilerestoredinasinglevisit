Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I get same day dentures?

Most patients receive their completed immediate dentures or full dentures within a single visit, depending on their individual case and any needed tooth extraction beforehand.

Do you offer emergency dental appointments?

Yes, we provide emergency dental care for broken dentures, sudden tooth pain, and urgent tooth extraction needs in Albany, NY.

What is the difference between full dentures and partial dentures?

Full dentures replace an entire arch of missing teeth, while partial dentures fill in gaps when some natural teeth remain healthy and in place.

Can dental implants support my dentures?

Dental implants can anchor full dentures or partial dentures, offering improved stability, comfort and confidence when chewing or speaking.

Do you provide cosmetic dentistry options?

Yes, our Albany office also offers cosmetic dentistry, dental veneers and clear braces for patients seeking both function and a refined appearance.
